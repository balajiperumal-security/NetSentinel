Prototype transparency

This build includes a heuristic detector, explainable risk scoring, a local rule-based assistant, event search, CSV export, and text incident reports. It does not contain a trained machine-learning model, connect to a live network capture feed, or automatically block traffic. Sample events are simulated. Only analyze systems you own or are authorized to test.
